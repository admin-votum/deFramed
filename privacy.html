Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

deFramed ("we", "our", the "site") is a small publishing site made of video windows, images, fragments, and notes. This policy explains what information is processed when you visit the site.

Information We Do Not Collect

deFramed is designed for privacy:

  • We do not require accounts or logins
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or personal identifiers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell or share visitor data with advertisers
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs

Local browser storage

The optional preview editor can save header text and windows in your browser's local storage. These edits are not sent to us and do not change the public site for other visitors. A video selected from your device is available only for that browser session unless it is separately uploaded to the site's media folder by the site owner.

External video providers

Some windows may embed or link to videos hosted by third parties such as YouTube, TikTok, or another media host. Those providers may process requests according to their own privacy policies when a video is loaded or played.

AI-Generated Content

Some captions or notes may be generated with third-party artificial intelligence services. When that happens, only the content needed to produce the requested text is sent to the relevant provider:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — privacy policy
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) — privacy policy
  • Google (Gemini) — privacy policy
  • DeepSeek — privacy policy

No personal information about you is intentionally included in these requests. AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies and is provided for general information.

Technical Data

Our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser details for security and operational purposes. This information is subject to the provider's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

deFramed is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner.
